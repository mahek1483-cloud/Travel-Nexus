Government of India Guidelines

India e-Visa Eligibility & Advisory

Plan your domestic Indian journey with confidence. Check requirements, validity types, and access the official Ministry of Home Affairs portal directly.

✅
Eligible for India e-Tourist Visa
Standard processing turnaround: 72 hours (3 business days)
30-Day e-Tourist Visa

Double entry valid for 30 days from date of first arrival in India.

Jul–Mar: $25 / Apr–Jun: $10
1-Year e-Tourist Visa

Multiple entries, max continuous stay of 90-180 days per visit.

Fee: ~$40 USD
5-Year e-Tourist Visa

Multiple entries across five full calendar years for frequent travelers.

Fee: ~$80 USD

Mandatory Application Documents

  • ✓ Passport must have at least 6 months validity from arrival date and 2 blank pages.
  • ✓ Clear digital scan of passport biographical page (PDF or JPG format).
  • ✓ Recent passport photo (square, plain white background, front-facing).
  • ✓ Return flight ticket or onward domestic journey itinerary.
⚠️
Official Advisory: Avoid Unofficial Agency Scams

Dozens of clone websites charge travelers $150–$300 for basic form submissions. The Government of India only operates one official website at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Travel Nexus does not charge any visa processing fee and directs you straight to the authentic portal.
